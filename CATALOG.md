# Book catalog

## PWS HexCell

**Full title:** The Complete Working Inventions of Robert Petrušić — Book II: PWS HexCell  
**Inventor and originating author:** Robert Petrušić  
**Edition:** Working revision 0.1, 4 August 2026  
**Editorial credit:** English technical reconstruction and document engineering in collaboration with ChatGPT, as stated in the source edition.

This technical book describes a recycled-polymer block concept, an experimental layered mould, production and quality-control steps, and a proposed first one-square-metre wall test. It separates the photographed, hand-scale printed form from the still-unproven material, mould, production and building-performance claims. The printed form does not establish the polymer composition, structural capacity or suitability for construction.

## Lunar Industrial Base and Autonomous Robotic Infrastructure

**Concept architecture:** Robert Petrušić  
**Edition:** Technical edition 1.0, 4 August 2026

An engineering concept for a staged lunar infrastructure network, including relay spacecraft, surface robots, site preparation, a protected base, mining and processing. The edition distinguishes established physical and technical foundations from integrations requiring development and longer-term possibilities. It does not claim the complete system has been built or qualified for lunar operation.

## LITHOS / SAMOREPLIKA — Earth Development Plan Before the Moon

**Concept owner:** Robert Petrušić  
**Edition:** Technical planning version 0.1, 4 August 2026

A terrestrial development plan for modular robot kits, home fabrication, a game-linked engineering process and progressively controlled field testing. The proposed first goal is partial functional self-expansion through making and verifying suitable non-critical plastic parts. The edition does not claim complete self-replication.
